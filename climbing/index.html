    Rules and phases

    Version 5: more strength and muscle, not more random fatigue. Sunday is redpoints, Tuesday is route circuits with heavy grip and pulling, Thursday is hard repeats with campus and pull-up power. Every gym day ends with three core exercises.

    Every gym day

    • At least 60 minutes of pure wall climbing first, about 70 or more on most days. Every Beastmaker, campus, pull-up, fix and core drill comes after it.
    • No TRX on climbing days. Optional home TRX on Monday or Friday only; Wednesday and Saturday are rest.
    • Gym grades are your gym's 1-8 scale: grade 4 comfortable, grade 5 moderate to hard, grade 6 your project. Don't convert them to Font grades.
    • A true 4×4 is four problems repeated for four rounds. Five problems for four rounds is a 5×4. Rest 15-30 sec between problems and 3-4 min between rounds.
    • Aim for roughly 150-175 minutes in total per gym session.

    Campus

    • Campus is never part of pure climbing. Use grade 2-3 routes on big secure jugs: 3-5 ascents of 4-6 hand moves, 2.5-3 min rest.
    • Slow, clean and controlled. No max leaps or swinging. Stop when accuracy drops, or switch to feet-on.

    When to change the load

    Increase

    After two clean sessions at or under the prescribed RPE, change ONE of weight, difficulty, reps or seconds. On the 20 mm edge, +1-2.5 kg; never change weight, time and edge together.

    Repeat

    Effort above the target: repeat the same sets and load next time instead of chasing failure.

    Reduce

    Recovery getting worse: eat more and cut the workload. Deload weeks 4 and 8 are never failure days.

    Use the Load Log to make these calls from what you actually did. Most muscle-building sets target RPE 7-9 with 1-3 clean reps in reserve, with strict form on weighted pulls.

    Fingers and joints

    • Fingers and elbows adapt more slowly than muscles. Progress 20 mm and campus volume conservatively, even when you feel stronger.
    • Your 10 mm 3-finger pocket hangs were easy. That's no reason to max out pockets when tired.
    • Stop for sharp joint pain, sudden weakness or instability, and consider a professional reassessment if it recurs. One pain-free day isn't permission for maximal campus jumps.

    The 12 weeks

    Nutrition

    • Protein about 120-150 g a day, with carbohydrate around gym sessions.
    • Fuel high-effort sessions. Avoid an aggressive deficit while building muscle; the old 68-69 kg target no longer applies.
    • If recovery gets worse, eat more and reduce the workload.

    Load log

    What actually got harder. Log added weight, pull-ups, fix seconds, campus ascents, core and RPE inside each gym day. Tap a session to open it.

    What RPE means

    RPE stands for Rate of Perceived Exertion. It's a scale from 1 to 10 that measures how hard an exercise feels to you.

    In this program, RPE controls training intensity, especially for fingerboarding, pull-ups, lock-offs and strength exercises.

    RPE measures effort, not pain. Pain, or feeling worse the next day, always takes priority over the RPE target.

    Post-climbing fingerboard work is never taken to failure. If a hang feels harder than its target RPE, remove added weight or stop the set. For timed hangs, RPE is a rough effort estimate.
